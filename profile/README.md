### What we do

We are a multidisciplinary team of electronics engineers and designers covering the full stack, from silicon to enclosure. One technical partner for the whole product cycle: we do not resell or represent brands, we develop and manufacture our own devices.

| Area | Capabilities |
|---|---|
| **Firmware & embedded systems** | C/C++, bare-metal and RTOS (FreeRTOS, ESP-IDF, Zephyr), embedded Linux (Buildroot). ARM Cortex (STM32, NXP, ESP32), PIC, AVR. CI/CD and automated testing |
| **Hardware & electronics** | PCB design from simple boards to high-speed multilayer, power electronics, battery-powered devices, sensors and precision instrumentation, circuit simulation |
| **Connectivity & IoT** | Wi-Fi, Bluetooth/BLE, LoRa/LoRaWAN, cellular, Ethernet and custom RF protocols. Real-time monitoring, dashboards and cloud integration |
| **Industrial & mechanical design** | 3D enclosures and assemblies, design for manufacturing, 3D printing (FDM/resin) and injection molding, UX and ergonomics |
| **Reverse engineering** | Analysis, replication and improvement of third-party hardware, including complex instrumentation |
| **Lab & manufacturing** | In-house lab (oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, programmable supplies, power measurement, environmental testing), supply chain management, PCB fabrication and assembly |

### How we work

1. **Research & feasibility** — we shape the idea and verify it is viable.
2. **Roadmap** — requirements, scope, timeline, development cost and technology choices.
3. **Development** — design and iteration until the result meets the goal.
4. **Prototyping** — a functional prototype to test and validate the design.
5. **Production** — we handle series manufacturing, so you deal with a single supplier.
6. **Quality** — lab and field testing at every iteration.

### MoTI — industrial monitoring & telemetry

[MoTI](https://mateware.com.ar/en/moti/) is our own IIoT platform: a 24/7 digital supervisor. A device connects to the sensors a plant already has (or ones we design for it) and streams data in real time to our cloud platform, where you get history, statistics and remote control of actuators.

- **Energy** — power alerts, consumption efficiency, metering per line, machine or area.
- **Telemetry** — remote and scheduled control of pumps, motors and wells, including solar-powered LoRa links in the field.
- **Manufacturing** — real-time production count, downtime logging by cause and instant OEE per machine, operator and work order.

Non-invasive, connectivity-agnostic (Wi-Fi, cellular, LoRa, Ethernet), with predictive maintenance and an AI assistant that answers questions about your production and consumption.

MoTI has reached **technology readiness level 8 (TRL 8)**.
